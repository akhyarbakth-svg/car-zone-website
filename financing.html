Ways to pay

Pick the plan that fits

Not sure which is right? Our finance team will talk you through it — no pressure.

Hire Purchase (HP)

Spread the full cost into fixed monthly payments. Once the last one's paid, the car is yours.

  • Own it outright at the end
  • No mileage limits
  • Terms from 24 to 60 months
Most popular

PCP

Lower monthly payments with a final balloon payment. At the end, pay it and keep the car, hand it back, or part-exchange.

  • Lowest monthly cost
  • Three choices at the end
  • Agreed annual mileage

Pay in full

Pay by bank transfer or debit card, with or without a part-exchange. Simple, quick, no paperwork.

  • No interest to pay
  • Reserve online for £99
  • Drive away same day

Questions

Finance, answered

Still unsure? Call our finance team on 0161 496 0742.

Will checking my eligibility affect my credit score?

No. We use a soft search, which only you can see. A full search happens only if you go ahead with an application.

How much deposit do I need?

Anything from zero upwards. A bigger deposit lowers your monthly payment and the total interest you pay.

Can I part-exchange my current car?

Yes — its value can go towards your deposit, even if there's finance still owed on it.

Can I pay off my agreement early?

Yes, at any time. You'll get a settlement figure that includes a rebate on future interest.

What do I need to apply?

Your driving licence, three years of address history and your employment details.
