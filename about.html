Our story

It started with one forecourt and twelve cars

We opened on Kingsbridge Road because buying a car felt harder than it should. No hidden fees, no pressure — just good cars, properly checked, at a price on the windscreen that doesn’t move.

Today we’re a team of 34, with a workshop, a finance desk and customers who come back for their second, third and fourth car.

  1. 2014 Doors openOne forecourt, twelve cars and three people.
  2. 2017 Workshop opensServicing, MOTs and our 150-point inspection, all in-house.
  3. 2020 Going electricEV specialists, battery health reports and on-site charging.
  4. 2023 Buy onlineReserve, finance and arrange delivery without leaving home.
  5. Today 250+ cars, 18 brandsAnd the same promise we started with.
